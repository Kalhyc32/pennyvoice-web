Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Penny is a personal, independent voice assistant app. This page explains what data the app accesses, why, and what happens to it. Penny is built around one rule: it only ever acts when you directly ask it to — nothing runs, listens further, or sends data anywhere on its own initiative.

Who operates Penny

Penny is developed and operated by an independent developer as a personal project. For GDPR purposes, this individual is the data controller for the data Penny processes — the contact below is also the contact for the controller (info@pennyvoice.com).

Legal basis for processing

Penny processes your data on the basis of your consent — given when you connect a Google or Spotify account, grant Penny permission to use the microphone, contacts, location, or camera on your phone, or simply use the app. You can withdraw consent at any time (see "Your rights under GDPR" below) - Penny then immediately stops processing the relevant data.

Google Account data

If you choose to connect a Google account, Penny requests the following permissions:

  • Calendar events — to read your schedule and set entries when you ask it to, e.g. "what's on my calendar tomorrow."
  • List of your calendars — so Penny can also check schedules on calendars shared with you (e.g. a family calendar), not just your own primary one. Penny only reads this list to know where to look for events — it doesn't change which calendars you have or their sharing settings.
  • Google Tasks — to save voice notes and manage a shopping list you dictate.
  • Drive (app-created files only) — Penny can only see and manage files it creates itself, never any other file in your Drive. This is used solely to optionally upload a diagnostic log when you ask for one.

Penny never requests broader access to your Google account than this.

Spotify account data

If you connect Spotify, Penny can control playback (play, pause, skip, search, volume) on your behalf. It does not access your listening history, saved library, or any personal data beyond what's needed to control playback.

Data on your device

  • Microphone — a small on-device model listens locally for the wake phrase. Nothing is sent anywhere until the wake phrase is detected or you press to talk — at that point, your speech is sent to cloud services (Google Speech-to-Text and Gemini) to be understood and answered.
  • Contacts — read on your device only, to match a spoken name to a phone number when you ask to call or text someone. Contact data is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Location — used only to provide directions when you ask for navigation.
  • Camera — used only when you explicitly ask Penny to look at something. The photo is sent once to Google Gemini for an answer and is not stored by the app afterward.
  • Notifications — Penny can read your notifications aloud, but strictly only when you ask it to. It never reads, stores, or acts on notification content on its own.

Where things are stored

Account sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on your device (Android's secure keystore) and are used only to talk directly to Google's or Spotify's own servers on your behalf — Penny does not run its own backend server, and tokens are never sent anywhere else. App preferences (language, app color, saved parking location, etc.) are stored as plain files on your device only.

How long Penny keeps data

Sign-in tokens and app preferences stay on your device until you disconnect the app or uninstall it. Penny also gradually builds a short list of facts about you (e.g. names of people close to you, preferences) to help it answer more accurately - this also stays on your device only. Diagnostic logs that Penny backs up to your own Drive at your request are not removed by the app itself - they stay there until you delete them.

Third-party processing

To understand and respond to what you say, your requests are sent to Google's Gemini API. Speech recognition and voice output use Google Cloud's speech APIs. These providers process the request text/audio needed to answer you; Penny does not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else, and nothing is sold.

International data transfers

Speech recognition, Gemini's responses, and voice output are provided through Google's cloud services, which may process your request on servers outside the European Union/EEA. This processing takes place under Google's own standard contractual clauses and certifications for transferring data outside the EU.

Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict the processing of, port, and object to the processing of your data. Because Penny doesn't run its own server and keeps your data directly on your phone and in your own Drive, you already have direct access to it:

  • Your Google or Spotify account can be disconnected at any time from within the app, which revokes Penny's access immediately - because Penny doesn't run its own server, there's no separate copy of your data to delete elsewhere.
  • The long-term notes Penny gradually builds about you (e.g. names of people close to you, preferences) are deleted by clearing the app's data in your phone's settings (Settings → Apps → Penny → Storage) or by uninstalling the app.
  • Diagnostic logs backed up to your own Drive are not deleted by Penny itself - they stay there until you delete them directly on Drive.

If you need help with any of this, contact the email below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority - in the Czech Republic this is the Office for Personal Data Protection, or the equivalent authority in your own EU member state.

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact info@pennyvoice.com.

Changes to this policy

If Penny's features change in a way that changes what data it accesses, this page will be updated accordingly.

Penny is an independent, personal project — not affiliated with Google or Spotify.
